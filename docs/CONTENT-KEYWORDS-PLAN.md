# Content & Local Keywords Plan — Siva Pest Control

Goal: Improve local organic visibility in Hyderabad, Chennai, and Bangalore for high-intent service queries and informational blog traffic.

Target pages
- Homepage: target brand + service area (e.g., "Siva Pest Control — Hyderabad | Termite, Cockroach & Rodent Control")
- Service pages: one per service (termite control, rodent control, cockroach treatment, bed bug treatment, commercial pest control)
- Location pages: Hyderabad, Chennai, Bangalore (and key suburbs) — each location page should include local schema, NAP, service list, testimonials, and contact CTA.
- Blog: topical educational posts addressing seasonal peaks, prevention tips, and local pest concerns.

Keyword focus (high intent)
- Hyderabad: "termite control Hyderabad", "pest control in Hyderabad", "cockroach treatment Hyderabad"
- Chennai: "termite treatment Chennai", "rodent control Chennai", "pest control Chennai home"
- Bangalore: "pest control Bangalore", "white ant treatment Bangalore", "commercial pest control Bangalore"

Keyword focus (informational / long-tail)
- "how to get rid of termites in house" + city
- "best pest control near me" + city
- "cockroach prevention in monsoon" + city
- "cost of termite treatment in Hyderabad"

Content pieces to publish (first 3 months)
1. Pillar: "Complete Guide to Termite Control in [City]" (long-form, 2200+ words)
   - Target: `termite control [city]`, internal links to service page and pricing/contact.
2. Seasonal: "Monsoon Pest Prevention: Top Tips for South India Homes"
   - Target: monsoon-related queries; link to pest inspection CTA.
3. Local case study: "How we cleared a severe termite infestation in [Neighborhood], [City]"
   - Target: local intent + trust signals (before/after images, testimonial, timeline).
4. FAQ cluster: "Does pest control harm pets and children?" (DPDP + safety focus)
5. Pricing guide: "Termite Treatment Cost in [City] — What to Expect"

On-page SEO checklist per page
- Unique title and meta description including primary keyword and city.
- H1 with primary keyword, H2s covering benefits, process, FAQs, testimonials.
- Local Schema: `LocalBusiness` / `Service` / `FAQ` where applicable (we already generate these via `src/lib/seo.ts`).
- Contact CTA above the fold with phone and lead form.
- Fast images (properly sized + WebP), responsive, with descriptive `alt` text containing keywords when natural.
- Internal linking from homepage and relevant blog posts.

Technical & CRO suggestions
- Ensure location pages include Google Maps embed and NAP structured data.
- Add trust signals: BBB, local registrations, photos of technicians, customer reviews, guarantees.
- Add CTA variants: Click-to-call, WhatsApp, and 'Request Inspection' micro-form.

Measurement & KPIs
- Organic sessions for target keywords (GA4 + GSC impressions/clicks)
- Number of leads attributed to organic search
- Rankings for primary keywords (weekly checks)
- Rich results appearance for FAQ, Breadcrumb, and Article

Execution plan (first 90 days)
- Week 1: Publish 2 location pages (Hyderabad — north/south variants) + homepage refresh.
- Week 2: Publish Termite pillar + link to service/location pages.
- Week 3: Publish Monsoon prevention article + promote via WhatsApp groups/local forums.
- Week 4–12: Publish one case study + one FAQ/pricing article every 2 weeks.
